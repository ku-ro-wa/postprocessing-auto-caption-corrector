Find and fix the words your auto-captions got wrong.

For YouTubers who fix their own auto-captions: it flags the names, jargon and mishearings and proposes the correction, so you review a short list instead of re-reading the whole video.

Works with any SRT or VTT file.

How it works

  1. Upload your SRT or VTT file. It is scanned at once for words that look misheard.
  2. Correct runs a Read-through: an LLM reads the transcript, judges each Flag and raises the ones the scan missed.
  3. Review and export: accept or reject each Flag, then export the corrected file.
