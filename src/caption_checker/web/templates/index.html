Find and fix the words your auto-captions got wrong.

For YouTubers who fix their own auto-captions: it flags the names, jargon and mishearings and proposes the correction, so you review a short list instead of re-reading the whole video.

Works with any SRT or VTT file.

How it works

  1. Download your video's auto-captions from YouTube Studio.
    How to download them

    In YouTube Studio: Subtitles → pick the video → Edit next to the language → Options → Download subtitles.

    Menu names as of October 2026.

  2. Upload the file here. It is scanned at once for Flags (words or phrases that look misheard). Add the video link too if you like, to play each one while you review.
  3. Run the AI read-through: optional but recommended. The local scan alone is hit-or-miss; the AI reads your whole captions file and catches mistakes the scan can't see. {% if has_server_key and limits %} It's free up to the Free tier's Allowance of {{ limits.allowance_words|thousands }} words in any 24 hours, or you can use your own OpenRouter key. {% elif has_server_key %} It's free on this server, or you can use your own OpenRouter key. {% else %} This server has no Free tier, so it needs your own OpenRouter key. {% endif %} Nothing is sent to an AI until you ask for it on the review page: running the AI read-through sends its text to OpenRouter's model provider.
  4. Review the Flags: accept or reject each one.
  5. Export the corrected file and upload it back to YouTube Studio. {% include "partials/upload_back.html" %}
